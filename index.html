2017

OUR BEGINNING

A Small Beginning. A Meaningful Dream.

What began as a small college canteen in Ballari in 2011 took a new shape in 2017, when we opened the doors to our first Hotel Atithi restaurant.

It was built around simple food, sincere hospitality and the joy of seeing a guest leave satisfied. Every plate we served and every person we welcomed helped shape the values that would one day become Hotel Atithi.

2011 — We started with a reason to serve.
2017 — The Canteen Became a Restaurant, and the Dream Grew Bigger.
2021

OUR JOURNEY

Growing Through Your Trust.

As the years passed, the support of our Atithis encouraged us to dream a little bigger.

Our journey moved from the small canteen to Hotel Atithi on Infantry Road in Ballari, welcoming more families and becoming a part of their everyday dining experiences.

Soon, that trust travelled beyond our restaurant. Through catering, Hotel Atithi became part of birthdays, family functions, gatherings and some of life's most meaningful celebrations.

2021 — Our Atithis gave us the confidence to grow.
2024

A NEW CHAPTER

Bigger Dreams. The Same Heart.

In December 2024, another chapter of the Hotel Atithi journey began.

Our new space brought together a premium pure vegetarian dining experience, an A/C party hall and a larger vision for catering and celebrations.

The surroundings became more refined, the possibilities became greater and our ambitions continued to grow — but the reason behind everything remained exactly the same.

2024 — The dream became bigger, while the heart stayed the same.

Yet, our greatest achievement has never been measured by how much we have grown.

It is measured by how many people have made Atithi a part of their lives.

Every family that returned, every celebration we hosted, every meal shared and every Atithi who trusted us became part of our story.

Today, our spaces may be bigger, our experiences more refined and our dreams even greater — but our heart remains the same.

We want every Atithi who walks through our doors to experience something familiar, warm and genuine.

Something that feels like a home.

Where Every Customer Becomes Our Atithi. Where Every Atithi Feels Like a Home.
